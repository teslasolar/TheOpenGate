BUSPIRONE — 5-HT1A → GIRK K+ CHANNEL PATHWAY

5-HT1A Receptor-Regulated Signal Transduction Pathways in Brain
PMC / Cellular Signalling
PMC2903656 — 5-HT1A Signal Transduction
GIRK (Kir3) 5-HT1A Gi/Go R2 gate
Used for: canonical 5-HT1A → Gi/Go → Gbeta-gamma → GIRK (Kir3) channel opening pathway. K+ efflux causes neuronal hyperpolarization. Buspirone engages this pathway as a 5-HT1A partial agonist, meaning it is NOT K+ channel neutral — it opens GIRK channels, causing inhibitory hyperpolarization (opposite direction from K+ channel blockade).
